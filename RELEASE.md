Super Mario 64 (HarbourMasters' Ghostship) native for FakeR36S-OS on the fake R36S / GA36-MB (Allwinner A23, Mali-400). The zip is the port only, with nothing of the game in it: it plays your own ROM.

1. Unzip `FakeR36S-SM64-*.zip` into the root of the card's HOME partition. It adds `ports/sm64/` and its menu entry.
2. Copy your Super Mario 64 ROM (N64 USA or Japan, `.z64`, `.n64`, `.v64` or a zip of one, any file name) to the root of HOME.
3. On the handheld, open Ports, then Super Mario 64. The first start makes the game's files from your ROM (2 to 4 minutes on the A23, a counter shows progress; Select+Start waits until it is done), then the game starts.

Select+Start quits, Select+B shows the frame rate, Select+R1 / Select+L1 save and load a state. More in the [README](https://github.com/Eeman1113/FakeR36S-SM64#readme).
